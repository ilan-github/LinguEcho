## Day 1: "Ground Zero" (set up and send your first messages)

### Mission 1.1: Pick Your Battlefield (30 min)
- [ ] **Main market: UK and Ireland, worked remotely.** They're in the same time zone as Portugal, speak English, and pay 3–5x Porto prices.
  - [ ] Pick ONE trade: roofers, plumbers, electricians, landscapers, driveway/paving, cleaners, or locksmiths
  - [ ] Pick ONE mid-size city, e.g. Leeds, Bristol, Manchester, Cork, or Galway
  - [ ] Price: **£400 / €450**
- [ ] **Side market (optional): Porto tourism businesses that earn from foreign visitors**
  - [ ] Alojamento Local / Airbnb hosts who want a direct-booking site to avoid 15%+ platform fees
  - [ ] Tour, wine, and Douro experience operators; surf schools; boutique guesthouses
  - [ ] Expat-focused services: relocation agents, immigration lawyers, English-speaking real estate agents, clinics
  - [ ] Price: **€350–€500**
- [ ] Write it down: "I build websites for ______ in ______."

### Mission 1.2: Forge the Brand (30 min)
- [ ] Choose a simple studio name, e.g. "[City] Web Studio" or "SwiftSite"
- [ ] Create a free Gmail for the business, e.g. swiftsite.studio@gmail.com
- [ ] Use AI (Canva or ChatGPT) to make a simple text logo. No face or photo of you needed.
- [ ] Sign up for a free Loom account (for faceless screen-recording walkthroughs)

### Mission 1.3: Build the Weapon (90 min)
- [ ] Choose your site builder: **Framer**, **Durable**, **Wix AI**, or **Carrd** (pick one and stick with it)
- [ ] Use ChatGPT or Claude to write copy for a made-up business in your niche (Home, Services, About, Reviews, Contact)
- [ ] Build one polished demo site, mobile-friendly with a clear "Call Now" button
- [ ] Publish it and save the link. This is your portfolio.

### Mission 1.4: The Hunt (60–90 min)
- [ ] Create a Google Sheet with columns: Business | Owner name | Email | Phone | Current site | Problem | Status | Notes
- [ ] Search Google Maps for "[niche] in [city]"
- [ ] Add **150 businesses** that have no website, a broken or slow site, or an outdated design
- [ ] Find emails on their site, Facebook page, or Google Business Profile

### Mission 1.5: First Strike (60 min)
- [ ] Write your outreach message (template below)
- [ ] Send **50 messages** by email, Facebook DM, or Instagram DM
- [ ] Fill in the Day 1 scoreboard

## Day 2: "Mockup Blitz" (make it personal)

### Mission 2.1: Sniper Mode (2–3 hours)
- [ ] Pick your 10 best leads (the ones with no site or the worst sites)
- [ ] For each one, have AI build a quick **custom mockup** using their real name, services, and city (about 10–15 min each by duplicating your demo)
- [ ] Record a 60-second Loom of each mockup without your face: "Here's what [Business]'s new site could look like…"
- [ ] Send all 10 personalized mockups

### Mission 2.2: Volume Fire (1–2 hours)
- [ ] Add 100 more leads to the sheet
- [ ] Send **75 more** standard messages
- [ ] Reply to every response within 1 hour

### Mission 2.3: Payment Ready (20 min)
- [ ] Set up a way to get paid: **Stripe Payment Link**, **PayPal invoice**, or **Wise** (a Wise GBP account lets UK clients pay you like a local bank transfer)
- [ ] Portugal admin: open self-employed activity ("abrir atividade") on Portal das Finanças so you can issue legal invoices ("recibos verdes"). Confirm VAT exemption and tax details with a contabilista.
- [ ] Create a $350 payment link (or 50% deposit = $175)
- [ ] Write a one-paragraph scope: "3–5 pages, mobile-friendly, contact form, Google Maps embed, 1 round of revisions, live in 48 hours."

## Templates

### Cold outreach (Day 1+)
> **Subject:** Quick idea for [Business Name]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I was looking for [niche] in [City] and noticed [Business]'s website [doesn't load well on phones / looks a bit dated / isn't showing up]. Most customers search on their phones now, so that could be costing you calls.
>
> I put together a quick example of what a modern site could look like: [demo link]
>
> I can have yours live in 48 hours for $350 flat, with no monthly fees. Want me to make a free mockup with your business name on it?
>
> [Your first name]
> [Studio name]

### Personalized mockup (Day 2+)
> Hi [Name], I went ahead and built a free mockup of a new website for [Business]. Here's a 60-second walkthrough: [Loom link]
>
> If you like it, I can make it live in 48 hours for $350. Want it?

### Follow-up (Day 3+)
> Hi [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to send a free mockup for [Business], no strings attached. Interested?

### Closing (when they say yes)
> Awesome! Here's the payment link to lock in your spot: [link]. Once that's done, I'll send a quick form for your details and you'll have your site within 48 hours.

### Handling objections
- **"Too expensive"** → "I can do 50% now and 50% when you're happy with it."
- **"I already have a site"** → "Totally. The mockup shows how it could get you more calls on mobile. Want to compare?"
- **"Not right now"** → "No problem. Can I check back next month?" (add to follow-up list)

## Rules of Engagement
1. **Messages sent is the number that matters most.** Aim for 300–500 total this week.
2. **Reply fast.** Respond to every lead within 1 hour.
3. **Show, don't tell.** Mockups close deals.
4. **Ask for payment.** Include the payment link whenever someone shows interest.
5. **Use your own words.** Edit AI-written messages before sending so they don't sound robotic.
6. **Follow anti-spam laws.** Send personalized 1:1 messages only, no mass blasts, and stop messaging anyone who asks.
