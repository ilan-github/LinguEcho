# Operation School Pilot: 7-Day B2B Outreach Plan
**Business:** Linguecho for language schools. Students shadow short clips at home, get AI speaking feedback, and retell. Teachers see who practised. Comes with a printed **shadowing journal** carrying the school's logo.
**Offer:** A **4-week pilot with one class**, free or at a symbolic €49, that turns into a paid term license
**Target:** 100 schools listed, 10 discovery conversations, **3 signed pilots for the January term** by Day 7
**Rule:** Learn first, then sell. Every day ends with schools contacted and notes written down.

> **Timing:** Schools choose materials before each term starts. Pitch now (late Nov–Dec) for **January** pilots, and in **May–July** for **September**.

## Day 1: "Map the Market" (who buys, and where they are)

### Mission 1.1: Pick Your Segment (30 min)
- [ ] **Main market: private language schools in Lisbon, Porto, Braga and the Algarve.** There are many of them, you can visit in person, and the owner usually makes the decision.
  - [ ] Pick ONE language first: English, Spanish or Portuguese for foreigners
  - [ ] Pick ONE student type: adults, teens, or company classes
- [ ] **Side markets (later):**
  - [ ] Online schools and tutors teaching Brazilians English
  - [ ] University language centres
  - [ ] Corporate language trainers
- [ ] Write it down: "Linguecho helps ______ schools in ______ get students speaking between classes."

### Mission 1.2: Know the Buyer (15 min)
| Who | What they care about | Role in the sale |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner / director** | Keeping students, standing out from other schools, revenue per student | Signs and pays |
| **Director of studies (DOS)** | Results, exam pass rates, how teachers work | Recommends it, runs the pilot |
| **Teachers** | Less marking, motivated students | Use it every day. They can make it or kill it. |

### Mission 1.3: Build the Target List (60 min)

## Day 2: "Discovery Calls" (learn before you pitch)

### Mission 2.1: Book Conversations (1 hour)
- [ ] Send the **discovery request** (template below) to **40 schools**
- [ ] Call 10 schools and ask for the owner or the DOS: "I'm researching how schools handle speaking practice. Could I have 20 minutes of your time this week?"

### Mission 2.2: Walk-Ins (2–3 hours)
- [ ] Visit **5–8 schools** in person, mid-morning or early afternoon (between classes)
- [ ] Ask for 20 minutes now or a time later this week
- [ ] Run the Part 2 interview. **Don't show the product yet.**

### Mission 2.3: Notes (30 min)
- [ ] Fill in the school scorecard within 10 minutes of each conversation
- [ ] Record their exact words for: speaking homework, students dropping out, materials budget, and who decides

---

## Day 3: "Build the Demo" (show, don't tell)

### Mission 3.1: Demo Kit (2–3 hours)
- [ ] **3 demo clips** in the target language. Use **only footage you made yourself or licensed footage** (your own recordings, Creative Commons, stock, or AI-generated). No clips from films or TV series.
- [ ] A 60-second screen recording: clip, then the student shadows, then AI feedback, then the student retells, then the teacher dashboard
  - [ ] If the app isn't ready, show a mock-up (Figma, Canva or slides) and give the feedback yourself. This is fine for a pilot.
- [ ] A one-page PDF: problem, how it works, pilot offer, price after the pilot

### Mission 3.2: Journal Prototype (1–2 hours)
- [ ] Design a 20–30 page **shadowing journal** in Canva. Each page has a QR code to its clip, new words, a retelling box, and a self-score from 1 to 5.
- [ ] Put a demo school's logo on the cover
- [ ] Print **5 copies** at a local print shop or with a print-on-demand service. Target cost: €4–6 per copy.

### Mission 3.3: Keep Booking
- [ ] Send **30 more** discovery requests
- [ ] Hold 2–3 more discovery conversations

## Day 4: "Demo Day" (go back to the schools that showed the problem)

### Mission 4.1: Follow Up with Warm Schools (2–3 hours)
- [ ] Go back to the schools that told you about **pet peeves or workarounds** with speaking homework
- [ ] Use their own words: "Last time you told me that [their problem]. Here's what I built to fix that."
- [ ] Show the 60-second demo and hand them a printed journal with **their logo on it** (make it the night before)

### Mission 4.2: Make the Pilot Offer
- [ ] **4 weeks, 1 class, up to 15 students**, starting in January
- [ ] Free or €49. In return they agree to:
  - [ ] Have 1 teacher assign 2 clips a week
  - [ ] Join a 15-minute results meeting at the end
  - [ ] Let you use the results as a case study if they're good
- [ ] Agree on the **success measures** before you start (see the pilot agreement below)

## Market prices: what schools charge students

Know what a student pays before you name your price. Your bundle should be a **small add-on to the course fee, about 3–8%**.

### Published prices (2025/26)
| School | Course | Price |
|---|---|---|
| [Cambridge School](https://www.cambridge.pt/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Brochura-Aulas-Grupo-2025-2026-1.pdf) (Lisbon, Porto) | Group classes, monthly fee depending on lessons per week | **€78–388 a month** in person (€70–349 online), plus €85 registration |
| | Intensive course, 40 lessons, exam preparation (FCE/CAE) | €668–862 |
| | Portuguese for foreigners, 20 or 40 lessons, 3–6 students | €442 or €884 |
| [British Council](https://www.britishcouncil.pt/en/english/courses-adults/classes/programmes-pricing) (Lisbon, Porto) | Adult English course, 30 hours | **€612** (about €20 an hour) |
| [CIAL](https://www.learnportugueseinportugal.com/en/lisbon-school/courses/general-portuguese/group-courses/) (Lisbon, Porto, Faro) | Intensive Portuguese, 20 lessons a week, up to 8 students | **from €299 a week** |

### Typical prices by type of school (estimates, confirm in interviews)
| Type of school | Typical price | Per term (3–4 months) |
|---|---|---|
| Premium chains and institutes (Cambridge, British Council, IH, Berlitz, Wall Street English) | €150–250 a month for 2–3 hours a week | **€500–900** |
| Independent schools (Type A), mostly in Porto, Braga and the Algarve | €60–130 a month | **€200–450** |
| Kids and teens (Helen Doron, Fun Languages, Happy English) | €50–90 a month | €180–350 |
| Intensive Portuguese for expats (CIAL, Lusa, Portuguese Connection) | €250–350 a week | — |
| Private one-to-one lessons | €25–50 an hour | — |

On top of these, schools usually charge **€50–85 registration**, and the coursebook costs another **€30–60**. Prices in Lisbon are usually 15–30% higher than in Braga or the Algarve.

### What this means for your price
| School type | Student pays per term | Suggested Linguecho price per student per term | Share of the course fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent (Type A) | €200–450 | €15–25 | about 5–8% |
| Premium chain (Type B) | €500–900 | €30–40 | about 4–6% |
| Kids and teens | €180–350 | €12–20 (journal-led) | about 5–7% |

- **Retention:** with course fees of about €600 a term, keeping **one** student who would have left covers Linguecho for about 15–24 students. Make this the main point of your pitch.
- **In each interview:** ask what students pay per term (Part 2, Step 5) and write it in the "Notes" column of the sheet.

## Templates

### Discovery request (Day 2+)
> **Subject:** 20 minutes about speaking practice at [School]?
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm researching how language schools in [City] handle speaking practice between classes. I'm not selling anything. I'm trying to learn how schools like [School] do it today.
>
> Could I have 20 minutes of your time this week, in person or by video? Happy to share what I learn from other schools.
>
> [Your first name]
> Linguecho

### Pilot offer (Day 4+, only to schools that described the problem)
> Hi [Name], thanks again for your time. You mentioned that [their exact words, e.g. "students never do speaking homework"].
>
> I've built something to help with that: students shadow short clips at home, get AI feedback on their speaking, and your teacher sees who practised. Here's a 60-second demo: [link]
>
> Would you try it with one class for 4 weeks in January, [free / €49]? If speaking homework doesn't improve, you've lost nothing.

### Follow-up (Day 5+)
> Hi [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to come by for 20 minutes whenever suits you. I'm also sharing a short summary of what other schools told me about speaking practice.

### Pilot agreement outline (1 page)
1. **Parties:** [School] and [your name / Linguecho], with NIFs
2. **Scope:** 1 class, up to 15 students, 4 weeks, [start date]–[end date]
3. **Price:** free / €49, plus [n] printed journals at €__ each (optional)
4. **School commits to:** 1 teacher assigning 2 clips a week, plus a 15-minute results meeting
5. **Success measures** (agree on them at the start):
   - [ ] Speaking homework completion: from __% to __%
   - [ ] Students' speaking confidence (1–5 survey): before __ / after __
   - [ ] Teacher feedback: "Would you keep using it?" (Y/N)
6. **After the pilot:** if the measures are met, continue at €__ per student per term (or the school can stop, with no obligation)
7. **Data (GDPR):** voice recordings are used only to give feedback. Data is stored in the EU and deleted __ days after the pilot. A DPA is attached. For students under 18, the parents give consent.
8. **Case study:** the school agrees / doesn't agree to be named in a results summary
9. **Content:** all clips are self-produced or licensed. No copyrighted film or TV footage.

### Handling objections
| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We already use Duolingo / the coursebook's app." | "Those are for vocabulary and grammar. This is speaking homework your teachers can see, and it works alongside your coursebook." |
| "Teachers don't have time." | "It saves time: assigning a clip takes about 30 seconds, and the AI gives the first round of feedback." |
| "Students won't do it." | "That's exactly what the pilot measures. If completion doesn't go up, you don't pay." |
| "It's too expensive." | "Your students pay about €[300–900] a term. This is about 5% of that, and if it keeps just one student enrolled for one more term, it pays for the whole class." |
| "What about student data?" | "Data is stored in the EU, deleted after __ days, and covered by a DPA. Here's our privacy notice." |
